About me

I study computer and network engineering because I like understanding how things connect: from the cable and the PC to the router and the internet behind it.

My years in Islamic boarding school taught me routine, patience and self-discipline. I bring that to technical work: follow a method, test one thing at a time, and write down what fixed the problem.

How I troubleshoot

  1. Identify

    Ask what changed, then reproduce the problem so it can be seen.

  2. Isolate

    Test one thing at a time: cable, device, address, route.

  3. Fix

    Apply the smallest change that solves it, then test again.

  4. Document

    Write down the cause and the fix so next time is faster.
